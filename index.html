Meet the Baker

Hi, I'm Jannatul — welcome to my kitchen.

MOMO'S SWEET started with a whisk, a borrowed oven, and a lot of trial and error. I'm currently learning the art of cake-making, testing recipes, chasing the perfect crumb, and figuring out fondant one cake at a time.

Every order here is handled personally — mixed, baked, and decorated by me. It's small-batch, homemade, and made with genuine care, not a factory line. Thank you for being part of the journey while I learn and grow.
